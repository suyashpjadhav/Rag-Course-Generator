Using ERP for integrated Sales Management
By Jonathan Davies
When manufacturers think about installing ERP software, it is often linked to wanting to
make improvements to production management. While it is true that ERP can hugely benefit
the production processes, ERP solutions can benefit other areas of the business as well.
