governed by Section 51 of the CGST Act, 2017. ■■■■■■■■■■■■■ ■■ ■■■: TDS under GST is
applicable to the following entities: - Government agencies or specified persons. - Local
Authorities - Such category of persons notified by the Government - Public sector
undertaking, etc ■■■ ■■■■■■ ■■■■■ ■■■: - TDS is deducted at a prescribed rate, which
should not exceed 2% (1% each for CGST and SGST/UTGST) of the total value of the supply.
