What is a Work Order? A work order is a formalized document that provides
specific instructions for a maintenance, repair, or operational task.

It acts as a
communication tool between departments, ensuring that tasks are clearly defined,
authorized, and tracked from initiation to completion.

Work orders may contain the
following details, Task Details: A clear description of the job.

Chunk 10:
Assigned Personnel: Who
is responsible for executing the task.

Time Frame: When the task should start and its
expected completion date.

Chunk 12:
Resources Needed: Any tools, parts, or equipment required.

Safety Protocols: Any safety measures or precautions to be taken.
