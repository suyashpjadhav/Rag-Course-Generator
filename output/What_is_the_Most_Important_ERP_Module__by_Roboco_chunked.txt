Chunk 21:
The Evolution of ERP Systems
The evolution of ERP systems has been a journey of continuous transformation, adapting to
the changing needs of businesses and advancements in technology.
==================================================
Chunk 22:
The genesis of ERP can be
traced back to the 1960s, with the advent of inventory management and control systems.
==================================================
Chunk 23:
In
the 1970s, this concept evolved into Material Requirements Planning (MRP) systems,
focusing on manufacturing processes.
==================================================
Chunk 24:
The 1980s saw the expansion of these systems into MRP
II (Manufacturing Resource Planning), which integrated additional processes such as human
resources and finance.
==================================================
Chunk 25:
The term ‘ERP’ itself was coined in the 1990s, marking the
transition to systems that encompassed a wider range of business functions beyond
manufacturing.
==================================================
Chunk 26:
The advent of the internet and cloud computing in the 21st century has
further revolutionized ERP systems, making them more accessible, scalable, and cost-
effective. Today’s ERP systems are more agile, user-friendly, and capable of handling big
data, analytics, and mobile computing.
==================================================
Chunk 27:
This evolution reflects the shifting landscape of
global business, where adaptability, integration, and real-time data are key to
maintaining a competitive edge.
==================================================
