Chunk 2:
Financial analysis charters into the world of multi dimensional analysis and
evaluation of an entire organization through series of complex KPIs. Unlike other
processes where we try to solve a single unit of work — financial analysis can have
multiple dimensions and each needs a special process and procedure to quantify outcomes.
